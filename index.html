Frequently Asked Questions

Why is a cast iron skillet the best choice for cooking keto meats?

Cast iron retains heat exceptionally well and reaches much higher temperatures than stainless steel or non-stick pans. This intense, even heat creates the Maillard reaction — the browning process that gives steaks and chicken their delicious crust and deep flavor. Unlike non-stick pans, cast iron is completely non-toxic at high temperatures, which is crucial since keto cooking often involves high-heat searing with butter or tallow.

Is the Instant Pot worth it for a keto diet?

Absolutely. The Instant Pot is arguably the most valuable investment for a keto dieter. Its most keto-specific use case is making bone broth — a pressure cooker extracts collagen, gelatin, and minerals from bones in 2–3 hours versus 18–24 hours on the stovetop. It also makes perfect pulled pork, keto soups, and steamed vegetables with minimal effort, dramatically reducing meal prep time.

Do I really need to weigh my food on keto?

For beginners, yes — a food scale is strongly recommended. The keto diet requires keeping net carbs under 20–50g per day, and it is surprisingly easy to over or underestimate portion sizes, especially for high-fat foods like nuts, cheese, and oils. Using a digital scale for the first few months builds an intuitive understanding of portions that most experienced keto dieters eventually internalize. For long-term success and to break plateaus, returning to the scale periodically is always helpful.

What vegetables work best in a spiralizer for keto?

The best keto vegetables for spiralizing are zucchini ("zoodles"), yellow squash, cucumber, daikon radish, turnip, and broccoli stems. These are all very low in net carbs. Zucchini is the gold standard — it becomes perfectly al dente when quickly sautéed in butter or olive oil. Avoid spiralizing sweet potatoes, beets, or carrots for a strict keto diet, as their carb content is significantly higher.

Are these kitchen tools good for meal prep?

All five tools on this list are excellent for keto meal prep. The Instant Pot is the ultimate batch-cooking machine. The cast iron skillet can cook a massive amount of ground beef or chicken thighs at once. The digital scale ensures consistent portions. The spiralizer can prep a week's worth of zucchini noodles in one session. And the avocado slicer makes prepping multiple avocados for guacamole or fat bombs fast and safe.
